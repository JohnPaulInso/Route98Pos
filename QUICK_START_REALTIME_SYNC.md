# Quick Start: Real-Time Automatic Sync

## 🚀 Get Started in 3 Steps

### Step 1: Open App on Multiple Devices ✅
- Device A: Computer/Laptop
- Device B: Phone/Tablet
- Both must be connected to internet

### Step 2: Login on Both Devices ✅
- Use same account (Owner/Admin or Rosella/Niño)
- Wait 5-10 seconds for sync connection
- Look for green sync indicator in top bar

### Step 3: Test It! ✅
- On Device A: Edit any product price
- On Device B: Watch it update automatically
- You should see: "Inventory updated from another device"

---

## ⚡ That's It!

Your devices are now synced automatically. Every change you make will appear on all devices instantly.

---

## 🎯 What Syncs Automatically

✅ **Add Product** → Appears on all devices  
✅ **Edit Product** → Updates on all devices  
✅ **Delete Product** → Removes from all devices  
✅ **Adjust Stock** → Stock updates everywhere  
✅ **Complete Sale** → Sales synced across devices  
✅ **Open/Close Shift** → All devices see shift status  

---

## 🔔 Notifications You'll See

When another device makes changes, you'll see:

- 📦 "Inventory updated from another device"
- 🔄 "Shift opened on another device"
- ✅ "Shift closed on another device"

---

## 💡 Tips

1. **Wait 10 seconds** after opening app for connection
2. **Check green indicator** in top bar = connected
3. **Red indicator** = not connected (check internet)
4. **Changes take < 1 second** to sync

---

## 🐛 Not Working?

### Quick Fix:
1. Refresh page (F5) on both devices
2. Wait 10 seconds
3. Try making a change again

### Still Not Working?
- Check internet connection on both devices
- Go to Settings → Data & Backups
- Click "Sync Now"
- Check browser console (F12) for errors
